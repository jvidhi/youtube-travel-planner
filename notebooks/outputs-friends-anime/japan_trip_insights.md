### Extracted Trip Insights (Know Before You Go)

Here are the actionable trip insights extracted from the travel video recommendations, organized by category:

### **Budgeting, Transit, & Accommodation**
*   **Opt for Social Hostels:** Save on accommodation costs in Tokyo and Osaka by booking social hostels.
*   **Hack Your Flights:** Use the **JAL (Japan Airlines) free domestic flight hack** to travel to regional areas like Kyushu on a budget.
*   **Minimize Transit Costs:** Balance your daily budget by actively researching and choosing the cheapest local transit options.

### **Tokyo & Osaka Sightseeing & Dining**
*   **Find Cheap Eats in Dotonbori:** Head to Osaka's neon-lit Dotonbori district for cheap street food, specifically affordable *takoyaki* (octopus balls).
*   **Seek Out Budget Viewpoints:** Save money by looking for budget-friendly viewpoints around Osaka, such as the Umeda Sky Building.
*   **Prepare for Festivals (Kishiwada Danjiri Matsuri):** If you attend this high-energy festival, **wear highly comfortable walking/running shoes**—you can easily clock over 30,000 steps a day chasing the fast-moving, 4-ton wooden floats.

### **Minakami (Gunma) Adventure**
*   **Go Canyoning at Fox Canyon:** Book a canyoning trip here to experience extreme water sports, including cliff jumps, white-water rapids, and a massive 20-meter natural waterfall slide.
*   **Unwind at a Local Hangout:** Stop by the *I Love Outdoors Cafe* after your canyoning adventure for cheap snacks and a places to relax with friends.

### **Iya Valley (Shikoku) Exploration**
*   **Cross the Vine Bridge (Kazurabashi):** Walk across this suspension bridge made of mountain vines, but be prepared for a thrill—there are massive, heart-stopping gaps between the wooden planks.
*   **Visit the Scarecrow Village (Nagoro):** Stop by this unique village where life-sized scarecrows outnumber the human residents.
*   **See the Peeing Boy Statue:** Look for this quirky statue perched precariously on a 200-meter-high cliff edge.

### **Takachiho Gorge (Kyushu) Tips**
*   **DIY the Trip:** Avoid tour crowds at the gorge by planning a self-guided (DIY) itinerary.
*   **Eat Flowing Noodles (Nagashi Somen):** Try this fun, cheap, and interactive dining experience nearby, where you catch noodles flowing down a bamboo pipe with chopsticks.
*   **Explore Sacred Caves:** Take a short drive from the gorge to **Amano Iwato Shrine & Amanoyasukawara Cave** to see the legendary cave filled with thousands of stacked stones left by pilgrims.
*   **Ride the Amaterasu Railway:** Take a ride on this open-top, highly scenic railway that suspended 105 meters above the valley.
