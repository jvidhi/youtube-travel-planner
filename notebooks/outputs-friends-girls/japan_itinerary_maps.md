# Itinerary Overview and Route Flow

This 14-day itinerary is custom-designed for a 20-something group of friends or a bachelorette trip. It perfectly balances high-energy city nightlife, adrenaline-fueled nature adventures, immersive traditional festivals, and aesthetic stationery shopping. 

To prevent you from feeling rushed, this itinerary uses a **strategic geographic loop** and integrates a **luggage forwarding service (*Takuhaibin*)**. This allows you to travel light with just a backpack during your mountain and countryside transitions, leaving your heavy suitcases to meet you at your next major city hotel.

# 🧳 The Secret to a Stress-Free Group Trip: Luggage Forwarding
Before leaving Tokyo on **Day 5**, ask your hotel front desk to help you arrange **Takuhaibin** (Yamato Transport/Black Cat service). You can ship your large suitcases directly from your Tokyo hotel to your Osaka hotel for about ¥2,000–¥3,000 per bag. 
*   **Why this is a game-changer:** You will spend Days 5 to 9 traveling through the mountain towns of Gujo Hachiman and Minakami carrying only a light backpack with 4 days of clothes. You won't have to haul heavy bags up train stairs, onto buses, or squeeze them into overhead Shinkansen compartments! Your bags will be waiting in your Osaka hotel room when you arrive on Day 9.

# 🇯🇵 Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

## Day 1: Welcome to Tokyo – High-Energy Landing
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Land at Haneda (HND) or Narita (NRT) Airport. Grab your pocket Wi-Fi or activate your eSIM. Take the *Keikyu Airport Line* (from Haneda, ~30 mins) or the *Narita Express* (from Narita, ~60 mins) straight to Central Tokyo.
*   **Where to Stay:** Check into **MIMARU Tokyo Hatchobori** or **MIMARU Tokyo Station East**. This highly-rated, mid-budget apartment hotel chain is perfect for a girls' trip, offering spacious rooms with multiple twin beds, bunk beds, fully equipped kitchens, and dining areas so your group can stay together, pre-game, and split costs.
*   **Evening:** Ease into the Tokyo vibe. Head to **Shibuya** (take the Hanzomon Line directly to Shibuya Station). Walk across the famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing, grab a casual dinner of sizzling gyoza or ramen, and head back early to beat the jet lag.

---

## Day 2: The Aesthetic Stationery & Skyline Experience
*   **10:00 AM – Custom Notebooks at Kakimori (Kuramae):** Take the Asakusa Line to Kuramae Station and walk 8 minutes to **kakimori** (*1-6-2 Misuji, Taito City*). This chic shop is world-famous among stationery lovers. You will select your cover material (leather, felt, or Japanese paper), inner paper types (ruled, grid, sketch), and ring bindings. The staff will assemble your custom notebook on the spot. 
*   **12:30 PM – Hipster Kuramae Café Lunch:** Kuramae is Tokyo’s "Brooklyn." Grab an aesthetic lunch and craft coffee at a local spot along the Sumida River, then hop on the Ginza Line to Ginza Station.
*   **2:00 PM – 12 Floors of Paper Heaven at Ginza Itoya:** Walk 3 minutes from Ginza Station to **Ginza Itoya** (*2-7-15 Ginza*). Look for the giant red paperclip outside. This legendary 12-story stationery department store is a dreamscape. Each floor has a distinct theme—from high-end fountain pens and custom-mixed inks to an entire floor dedicated to thousands of varieties of texture-rich Japanese *washi* paper.
*   **5:00 PM – Sunset at Shibuya Sky:** Take the Ginza line straight to Shibuya. Enter Shibuya Scramble Square and ascend to **Shibuya Sky**, an open-air rooftop observatory 229 meters in the air. *Crucial Tip: You must book your tickets online exactly 4 weeks in advance to secure a golden hour sunset slot.* The 360-degree views of Tokyo (and Mount Fuji on a clear day) are stunningly photogenic.
*   **7:30 PM – Dinner & Drinks at CÉ LA VI:** Celebrate your first major night out at **CÉ LA VI TOKYO**, located on the 17th and 18th floors of Tokyu Plaza Shibuya. Grab dinner or cocktails on the outdoor terrace, which looks directly down onto the glowing neon lights of the Shibuya Crossing.

---

## Day 3: Digital Art & Shibuya Nightlife
*   **10:00 AM – Sensory Immersion at teamLab Planets (Toyosu):** Take the Yurakucho Line to Toyosu Station, then transfer to the Yurikamome Line to Shin-Toyosu Station. **teamLab Planets** is a barefoot digital art museum. You’ll wade through knee-deep warm water filled with projected koi fish, wander through an infinite dome of falling orchids, and lie under a shifting universe of digital flowers. *Wear shorts or pants that can easily be rolled up above the knee!*
*   **1:30 PM – Travel Journals at Traveler’s Factory (Nakameguro):** Take the Hibiya Line to Nakameguro Station and walk 3 minutes to **Traveler's Factory Nakameguro** (*3-13-10 Kamimeguro*). Housed in a retro paper-processing factory, this cozy shop is dedicated to customizable leather travel journals. Buy a notebook and spend an hour at their free stamping table, personalizing it with vintage travel stamps.
*   **3:00 PM – Meguro River Walk:** Grab an iced matcha latte and stroll along the scenic Meguro River canal, lined with trendy boutiques and indie clothing stores.
*   **9:00 PM – Girls' Night Out in Shibuya:** Start with private room karaoke at **Karaoke Kan Shibuya** (order drinks via tablet and sing your hearts out). If your group wants to dance, head to **WOMB** in Dogenzaka—a multi-story electronic music club with one of the largest disco balls in the world.

---

## Day 4: Vintage Thrifting & Quirky Tokyo Culture
*   **11:00 AM – Vintage Thrifting in Shimokitazawa:** Take the Keio Inokashira Line from Shibuya directly to Shimokitazawa Station (only 5 minutes on the express). This bohemian neighborhood is Tokyo’s vintage clothing capital. Spend the afternoon browsing incredible thrift stores like *Toyo Department Store*, *New York Joe Exchange*, and *Chicago*. 
*   **3:00 PM – Harajuku & Takeshita Street:** Take the Chiyoda Line to Meiji-jingumae Station. Walk down Takeshita Street to experience Tokyo’s colorful youth culture. Grab giant rainbow cotton candy, aesthetic Japanese crepes, and browse quirky cosmetics at **Loft Shibuya** (a multi-story lifestyle store packed with trendy stickers, planners, and skincare).
*   **7:00 PM – Retro Shinjuku Dinner:** Head to Shinjuku Station. Explore **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane) or **Golden Gai**—tiny, atmospheric alleyways packed with miniature bars and yakitori stalls. It’s highly photogenic and offers an authentic, old-school Tokyo nightlife experience.

---

## Day 5: Journey to Gujo Hachiman – The Gujo Odori Festival Finale!
*   **8:30 AM – Shinkansen to Nagoya:** Drop your large bags off at the hotel front desk for forwarding to Osaka. Hop on the Tokaido Shinkansen from Tokyo Station to Nagoya Station (1 hour 40 minutes). 
*   **11:30 AM – Highway Bus to Gifu’s Canal Town:** From Nagoya Station, walk to the adjacent Meitetsu Bus Center. Board the scenic highway bus directly to the **Gujo Hachiman Castle Town Plaza** (1 hour 30 minutes). 
*   **2:00 PM – Check-in & Yukata Fitting:** Check into a traditional riverfront inn like **Nakashimaya** or **Miharaya Ryokan**. Head to a local rental shop to be fitted for a traditional summer *yukata* (cotton kimono) and *geta* (wooden clogs). 
*   **7:00 PM – Join the Dance Circle at Gujo Odori:** September hosts the highly anticipated **Gujo Odori Festival Finale** (usually the first Saturday of September). Unlike other festivals where you merely watch, Gujo Odori welcomes tourists to join the dancing. Follow the simple steps of the locals, clack your wooden geta clogs on the stone streets, and dance under the lanterns. The atmosphere is warm, festive, and incredibly welcoming!

---

## Day 6: Wax Food Workshops & Pristine Canals
*   **10:00 AM – Hilarious Wax Food Workshop:** Walk to **Sample Village Iwasaki** (*250 Hachimancho Jonancho*). Gujo Hachiman is the birthplace of *sampuru*—the hyper-realistic plastic food models used in restaurant windows. Under the guidance of a master artisan, your group will use warm water and colored wax to craft your own wax lettuce heads, tempura shrimp, or ice cream. It is a highly-rated, hilarious group activity.
*   **1:00 PM – Explore Stone Canals & Igawa Lane:** Walk along the historic streets to **Igawa Lane** and **Yanaka Lane**. These narrow stone paths run alongside canals of crystal-clear mountain water where giant, colorful koi fish swim. Locals still use these pristine waters to wash vegetables.
*   **3:00 PM – Matcha Parfaits at Sogi-an:** Visit **Sogi Spring**, a historic water source. Right next to it is **Sogi-an** (*862-10 Hachimancho Honmachi*), a beautiful traditional cafe. Grab tatami seating overlooking the river and enjoy their famous matcha parfaits, soft-serve ice cream, and dango.
*   **6:30 PM – Hida Wagyu Dinner at Daihachi:** Dine at **Daihachi** (*883-1 Hachimancho Sakanamachi*), a highly-rated local restaurant. Try their melt-in-your-mouth Hida Wagyu beef bowls or local salt-grilled sweetfish (*ayu*) caught fresh from the Yoshida River.

---

## Day 7: Travel to Gunma's Adventure Capital
*   **9:00 AM – Scenic Mountain Transit:** Take the highway bus back to Nagoya (1.5 hours). Board the Shinkansen from Nagoya to Omiya Station (1 hour 45 minutes), then transfer to the Joetsu Shinkansen to **Jomo-Kogen Station** (1 hour). From there, take a local bus or taxi (25 minutes) to the mountain town of **Minakami**.
*   **3:30 PM – Check into an Onsen Ryokan:** Check into a traditional hot spring resort like **Ryokan Tanigawa** or a mid-budget inn along the rushing Tone River. 
*   **Evening – Ultimate Relaxation:** Put on your ryokan’s cozy yukata and slide into the outdoor hot springs (*rotenburo*). Soak in the mineral-rich, healing waters while listening to the wind in the trees and the rushing river below. Follow this with a multi-course traditional *kaiseki* dinner served directly to your room or dining hall.

---

## Day 8: Adrenaline Canyoning & River Rafting
*   **9:00 AM – Fox Canyon Canyoning with Canyons Japan:** Meet the team at **Canyons Japan** (*45 Yubiso, Minakami*). Strap on a thick wetsuit, harness, and helmet. You’ll hike into the stunning Fox Canyon, then slide down natural, polished stone waterslides, zip-line across deep gorges, and plunge off waterfalls—culminating in a heart-pounding **20-meter natural waterfall slide**! September is the absolute peak season for this, with warm air and refreshing mountain water.
*   **1:00 PM – BBQ Lunch:** Enjoy a hearty burger or BBQ lunch at the Canyons Alpine Café overlooking the river.
*   **2:30 PM – White-Water Rafting:** Take to the rapids of the Tone River. Work together as a team to navigate the thrilling white-water rapids surrounded by dramatic canyon walls.
*   **6:00 PM – Soothe Sore Muscles:** Head back to your ryokan for another long, luxurious soak in the outdoor onsen to soothe your muscles after a day of high-octane adventure.

---

## Day 9: Journey to Osaka – Street Food & Neon Lights
*   **10:00 AM – Shinkansen to Osaka:** Take a local bus/taxi to Jomo-Kogen Station. Board the Joetsu Shinkansen to Tokyo Station (1 hour 15 minutes), then transfer directly to the Tokaido Shinkansen to **Shin-Osaka Station** (2 hours 30 minutes). 
*   **3:00 PM – Check-in & Luggage Reunion:** Check into **MIMARU Osaka Shinsaibashi East** (or similar). Your forwarded suitcases from Tokyo will already be safely delivered and waiting in your rooms!
*   **6:00 PM – Neon Street Food Tour in Dotonbori:** Walk to the neon-drenched canal district of **Dotonbori**. Snap the mandatory group photo with the giant Glico Running Man sign. Walk the bustling streets and sample famous Osaka street food: *takoyaki* (molten octopus balls) from *Kukuru*, crispy *kushikatsu* (skewered fried meats and veggies), and savory *okonomiyaki* (cabbage pancakes). 

---

## Day 10: Universal Studios Japan – Halloween Horror Nights
*   **8:30 AM – Head to USJ:** Take the JR line to Universal-City Station (only 15 minutes from central Osaka). 
*   **Day – Super Nintendo World & Harry Potter:** Spend your day exploring the highly immersive *Super Nintendo World* (make sure to secure an Area Timed Entry Ticket via the USJ app as soon as you enter the park) and drinking Butterbeer in the *Wizarding World of Harry Potter*.
*   **6:00 PM – Halloween Horror Nights:** September kicks off USJ's famous Halloween season. As the sun sets, the park transforms. Hundreds of "street zombies" roam the park, performing synchronized dance routines (like the "Zombie de Dance" to high-energy J-pop) and chasing guests. It is an incredibly fun, high-energy party atmosphere perfect for a young group of friends!

---

## Day 11: Amemura Thrifting & Osaka's Wildest Nightclub
*   **11:00 AM – Thrift Shopping in America-mura (Amemura):** Walk to Amemura, the epicenter of Osaka's youth and street culture. It’s packed with vintage clothing stores, record shops, and cheap street food (like giant 40cm ice cream cones). Hang out around *Sankaku Koen* (Triangle Park) to people-watch.
*   **3:00 PM – Shinsaibashi Shopping & Café Hop:** Walk through the Shinsaibashi-suji shopping arcade. Stop at aesthetic cafes for soufflé pancakes or matcha lattes.
*   **10:00 PM – Party at THE PINK:** Experience Osaka's legendary nightlife. Head to **THE PINK** (*1-18-27 Higashishinsaibashi*), a wildly popular, high-energy nightclub styled entirely in neon pink. It has a fantastic bachelorette/girls' trip vibe with great pop/hip-hop music and go-go dancers. Alternatively, check out **GHOST Osaka** (*2-17-3 Nishishinsaibashi*) for a sleek, high-end hip-hop clubbing experience.

---

## Day 12: Kishiwada Danjiri Matsuri (Day 1) – Japan's Wildest Festival
*   **9:00 AM – Head to Kishiwada:** Take the Nankai Line from Namba Station directly to Kishiwada Station (approx. 25 minutes). 
*   **10:00 AM – Feel the Adrenaline of Danjiri:** Kishiwada Danjiri Matsuri is famous for being **Japan's most high-octane traditional festival**. Watch in awe as neighborhood teams of hundreds of locals pull massive, intricately carved 4-ton wooden carts (*danjiri*) through narrow streets at breakneck speeds. 
*   **The Highlight – Yari Mawashi:** Secure a safe spot near a street corner to witness the *Yari Mawashi*—where the teams corner these massive, heavy wooden floats at full speed, guided by a brave team leader dancing precariously on the roof of the moving cart. 
*   **Afternoon – Street Food Feast:** The streets are lined with hundreds of *yatai* (food stalls). Feast on yakitori, yakisoba, and ice-cold beers while cheering alongside thousands of locals. The energy is absolutely electric!

---

## Day 13: Danjiri Matsuri (Day 2) & Elegant Lantern Parade
*   **1:00 PM – Return to Kishiwada:** Head back to Kishiwada for the second day of the festival. The daytime runs continue with intense speed and energy.
*   **7:00 PM – The Lantern Parade (Illuminated Danjiri):** As night falls, the atmosphere shifts completely. The high-speed racing stops, and the danjiri floats are decorated with over 200 paper lanterns. They are pulled slowly and gracefully through the streets, accompanied by traditional festival music (gong, drums, and flute). It is a stunning, deeply cultural, and photogenic sight.
*   **9:30 PM – Final Celebratory Dinner:** Head back to Namba for a celebratory final dinner at a lively *izakaya*, toasted with highballs and draft beers to mark an unforgettable trip.

---

## Day 14: Final Souvenirs & Departure
*   **Morning:** Sleep in, pack your bags, and do some last-minute shopping at *Don Quijote Shinsaibashi* for unique Japanese snacks (KitKats, matcha sweets) to bring home.
*   **Afternoon:** 
    *   **If flying out of Osaka:** Take the sleek Nankai Rap:t Express train from Namba Station directly to Kansai International Airport (KIX) in just 35 minutes.
    *   **If flying out of Tokyo:** Hop on the Tokaido Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka back to Tokyo Station (2.5 hours) to catch your flight home.

# 💡 September Travel Tips & Weather Guide

1.  **September Weather:** Early September behaves like summer—expect warm, humid days ranging from 25°C to 32°C (77°F to 90°F). Pack light, breathable clothing, but bring a light jacket or cardigan for the air-conditioned trains and cooler mountain evenings in Minakami and Gujo Hachiman.
2.  **Typhoon Season Awareness:** September is peak typhoon season in Japan. While they rarely disrupt city travel for more than 24 hours, they can cause temporary train delays or outdoor activity cancellations. **Crucial Tip:** Ensure your canyoning activity with Canyons Japan has a flexible booking/cancellation policy, and keep a backup indoor activity (like an arcade day or spa visit in Osaka) in mind just in case.
3.  **Transit Passes:** Since this itinerary covers Tokyo, Gunma, Gifu, and Osaka, individual Shinkansen tickets are highly recommended, as they are often more cost-effective than the full JR Pass. Use the **SmartEX App** to book your Tokaido Shinkansen tickets (Tokyo to Nagoya, and Shin-Osaka to Tokyo) in advance to secure seats together.
