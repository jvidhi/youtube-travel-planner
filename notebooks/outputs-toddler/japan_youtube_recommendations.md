## 3. Know Before You Go (Essential Family Travel Tips)
*These videos are absolute must-watches before your trip. They cover the logistics, cultural etiquette, and transit hacks of traveling in Japan with young children.*

### 👶 Expat Mom's Guide to Japan with a Toddler
*   **Video Title:** [Traveling to Japan with a Baby, Toddler, or Infant? Must-Know Tips!](https://www.youtube.com/@heratlas)
*   **Channel:** *Her Atlas*
*   **Crucial Tips Covered:**
    *   **Train Fare Rules:** Children aged 5 and under ride public transportation (subways, trains, and bullet trains) entirely for free in Japan!
    *   **Department Store "Baby Rooms":** How to find pristine, dedicated family rooms in Japanese department stores equipped with private nursing booths, hot water dispensers for formula, and toddler-sized toilets.
    *   **Family Dining Hack:** Caitlyn explains why dining on the restaurant/food court floors of department stores and shopping malls is a lifesaver, as they are incredibly spacious and stroller-friendly.

### 🧳 Stress-Free Logistics & Hotel Strategies
*   **Video Title:** [These must-know Japan travel tips will help you travel stress-free as a family with young kids](https://www.youtube.com/KenshoQuest)
*   **Channel:** *Kensho Quest*
*   **Crucial Tips Covered:**
    *   **MIMARU Apartment Hotels:** A visual tour of why MIMARU hotels are the ultimate family stay, featuring fully equipped kitchens, in-room laundry, and spacious layouts.
    *   **Luggage Forwarding (Takkyubin):** Detailed instructions on how to use services like Yamato Transport to ship your heavy suitcases directly from hotel to hotel, leaving you hands-free to manage your toddlers and stroller on the trains.
    *   **Private Airport Transfers:** Why booking a private van transfer from the airport to your Tokyo hotel is worth every penny to avoid navigating crowded train stations with jet-lagged toddlers.

### 🚮 Diaper Etiquette & Public Transit Hacks
*   **Video Title:** [30 Tips on How to Travel to Japan with Young Children](https://www.youtube.com/@YellowProductions)
*   **Channel:** *Yellow Productions*
*   **Crucial Tips Covered:**
    *   **Diaper Trash Etiquette:** Public trash cans are incredibly rare in Japan, and throwing dirty diapers in public restroom bins is considered bad etiquette. Always carry small plastic bags to seal dirty diapers and dispose of them back at your hotel.
    *   **Taxis & Car Seats:** Legally, children are allowed to ride in Japanese taxis without a car seat. Taking a quick taxi can save you from carrying a stroller up massive subway stairs.
    *   **Stroller vs. Carrier:** While Tokyo is stroller-friendly if you map out elevator exits, historic towns like Takayama have bumpy gravel paths. Bringing a lightweight, easily foldable stroller alongside a comfortable baby carrier is highly recommended.
