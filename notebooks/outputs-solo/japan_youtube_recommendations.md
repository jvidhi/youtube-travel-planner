# 🏮 1. Top Travel Guides & Reviews
*These highly-rated videos provide comprehensive overviews, practical logistics, and stunning footage of the key regions on your itinerary.*

### **Kamikochi & Matsumoto (Nagano)**
* **Video:** [Day Trip to Kamikochi - Stunning Nature Spot | Nagano](https://www.youtube.com/@MarisAroundtheWorld)
* **Channel:** Maris Around the World
* **Why watch:** This guide is perfect for budget travelers planning to base themselves in Matsumoto. Maris walks you through the exact bus transfer logistics from Matsumoto, starting the hike at the pristine **Taisho Pond**, and walking the flat but rocky trail to **Myojin Pond** and **Kappa Bridge**. It also gives you a realistic look at the crowds you might encounter and the 500 yen cash-only entry fee for the sacred pond.

### **Iya Valley (Tokushima, Shikoku)**
* **Video:** [Trip to IYA 🇯🇵 / Explore Hidden Valley in Japan / Kazurabashi / Oboke Gorge](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Trip+to+IYA+Explore+Hidden+Valley+in+Japan+Planet+Journey+JP)
* **Channel:** Planet Journey JP
* **Why watch:** This 4K travel vlog beautifully captures the mystical, isolated vibe of the Iya Valley. It provides close-up footage of the terrifying gaps on the **Kazurabashi Vine Bridge**, the roaring waters of **Oboke Gorge**, and the humorously famous **Peeing Boy Statue** towering over the canyon. 

### **Kishiwada Danjiri Matsuri (Osaka)**
* **Video:** [Osaka's High-Speed Kishiwada Danjiri Festival (Behind the Scenes)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Osaka+High+Speed+Kishiwada+Danjiri+Festival+Nippon+TV)
* **Channel:** Nippon TV News 24 Japan
* **Why watch:** If you want to understand the raw, heart-pounding energy of the Danjiri Matsuri, this news-style feature is a must-watch. It shows the intense physical training, the adrenaline-fueled *Yarimawashi* (90-degree corner turns at full speed), and the local community spirit that powers this 300-year-old festival.

# 🏔️ 2. Authentic Travelers & Hidden Gems
*These videos are hosted by authentic creators who venture off the beaten path to show you the wild, adventurous, and quirky sides of your destinations.*

### **Togakushi (Nagano) — The Advanced Ridge Hike**
* **Video:** [Togakushi Shrine: Complete Guide & Full Pilgrimage Trail Hike](https://www.youtube.com/@im.mattguy)
* **Channel:** Matt Guy
* **Hidden Gems Highlighted:** While most tourists only walk the flat, 2km cedar-lined path to the Okusha (Upper) Shrine, Matt Guy takes you on the **full, highly advanced pilgrimage trail** up Mount Togakushi. 
* **The Adventure:** He documents the thrilling (and highly dangerous) **chain climbs**—where you must haul yourself up vertical rock faces using heavy iron chains—and the terrifying **knife-edge ridges** with sheer drops on both sides. *Note: Only attempt the summit trail if you are an experienced hiker with proper gear!*

### **Iya Valley (Shikoku) — Nagoro Scarecrow Village**
* **Video:** [Exploring Japan's Hidden Valley: Vine Bridges & Scarecrow Village](https://www.youtube.com/@maximostravels)
* **Channel:** Maximo's Travels
* **Hidden Gems Highlighted:** Maximo takes a deep dive into the remote village of **Nagoro**, famously known as the "Scarecrow Village". 
* **The Adventure:** Because of depopulation, a local artist has replaced departed residents with over 300 life-sized scarecrows. Maximo explores the eerie, silent, abandoned schoolhouse where scarecrow students sit at desks, and even hunts down the scarecrow effigy of British TV presenter James May. 

### **Minakami (Gunma) — Extreme Fox Canyon Canyoning**
* **Video:** [I Went Canyoning in MINAKAMI JAPAN [4K]](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=I+Went+Canyoning+in+MINAKAMI+JAPAN+Rattyboy)
* **Channel:** Rattyboy
* **Hidden Gems Highlighted:** Rattyboy documents his high-adrenaline experience canyoning down **Fox Canyon** in Minakami. 
* **The Adventure:** Showing the raw, unpolished reality of the sport, he slides backward down massive natural waterfalls, navigates rushing river currents, and leaps off a towering bridge into a deep mountain pool. It is the ultimate visual guide for a 20-something thrill-seeker.

### **Daisetsuzan (Hokkaido) — Peak Autumn Volcanic Trek**
* **Video:** [My Favorite Place in Hokkaido | Daisetsuzan National Park Vlog](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=My+Favorite Place+in+Hokkaido+Daisetsuzan+National+Park+Vlog+The+Alan+Ang)
* **Channel:** The Alan Ang
* **Hidden Gems Highlighted:** Alan Ang documents an incredible hike from **Asahidake to Kurodake**.
* **The Adventure:** This trek takes you past the steaming sulfur vents of Mount Asahidake (an active volcano and Hokkaido's tallest peak), around the reflective Sugatami Pond, and down into Sounkyo Onsen. If you time this for mid-to-late September, you will see the absolute first (and most dramatic) autumn foliage in all of Japan.

# 🎒 3. Know Before You Go (Crucial Tips & Budgeting)
*Essential watching for solo, budget-conscious travelers to ensure you don't waste money and are fully prepared for Japan's unique travel landscape.*

### **The 2026 Budget Roadmap**
* **Video:** [Don't Waste Money in Japan | Budget Travel Tips](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Don%27t+Waste+Money+in+Japan+Budget+Travel+Tips+Daniele+from+Rome)
* **Channel:** Daniele from Rome
* **Why watch:** Daniele breaks down exactly how to travel Japan on a budget. He discusses why the national JR Pass is no longer worth it post-price hike and explains how to utilize regional passes and overnight highway buses to save on both transit and accommodation. He also covers realistic daily costs for food (averaging $15–$35/day using convenience stores and local noodle shops) and accommodation.

### **The Real Cost of 30 Days in Japan**
* **Video:** [How Much Does It REALLY Cost To Travel Japan?](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=How+Much+Does+It+REALLY+Cost+To+Travel+Japan+World+Wild+Hearts)
* **Channel:** World Wild Hearts
* **Why watch:** Hosted by a young travel couple, this video provides a highly transparent, penny-by-penny breakdown of traveling through Japan. They share excellent tips on how to enjoy world-class experiences for free (like exploring historic neighborhoods and finding free city observatories) and how to balance your budget between high-octane paid activities and cheap, authentic local street food.
