## 💡 Practical Travel Tips
* **Safety**: Albania is considered extremely safe with a low crime rate. Common sense applies in cities (e.g., keeping belongings close). Solo female travelers and hikers will find well-marked routes and helpful locals.
* **Budget Travel**: Albania is budget-friendly. Meals are typically 5-8 USD, and mid-range accommodation is around 50 USD/night. Beers cost €1-3, and cocktails €4-7.
* **Tourism Development & Infrastructure**: Tourism is still developing. Expect "rough and ready" experiences with potentially poor roads in smaller towns and occasional slow service. There is ongoing construction. Public transport (buses) may be unreliable and not reach all hidden gems.
* **Exploring Local Beaches**: To avoid crowds, seek out smaller, local beaches outside popular areas like Ksamil, Himara, and Saranda. Examples include Filicuri Beach and Gjipe Beach.
* **Bees**: Albania has many bees, which can be an issue for those with phobias, especially around food. However, there are very few flies, indicating a healthy ecosystem.
* **Must-Try Albanian Food**: Recommended dishes include Byrek (meat pie), Fërgesë (cottage cheese and capsicum), Tavë Kosi (slow-cooked lamb in yogurt sauce), and Trileçe (a three-milk sponge cake dessert). It is suggested to avoid chicken as it is often overcooked.
* **Recommended Restaurants**: *   **Era (Tirana):** Traditional, highly recommended for its meat pie.\n*   **Lily's Homemade Food (Berat):** Traditional, intimate, requires booking via WhatsApp.\n*   **Manra (Saranda):** Traditional, noted for outstanding service and the opportunity to make dough in the kitchen.
* **Unmissable Experiences**: Highlights include hiking in the Albanian Alps (e.g., Valbona to Theth route), visiting the Blue Eye natural spring, an interactive play called "Albanian Nights" in Tirana, and enjoying sunset cocktails at Saranda Castle.
* **Currency & Payments**: The Albanian Lek (LE) is the main currency, but Euro is widely accepted. It's more affordable to pay in LE unless a price is quoted in Euro. ATM withdrawals typically incur a 500-800 LE (5-8 USD) fee.
* **Interacting with Locals**: Albanians are warmhearted and helpful; engaging in conversation and learning simple phrases like "Faleminderit" (thank you) is appreciated.
* **Transportation (Car Rental)**: Hiring a car (~€30/day) is recommended for exploring hidden beaches and sites like the Blue Eye. Confirm with rental companies that driving on dirt tracks is allowed. Note that petrol is called "benzene." Skyline car rental is explicitly recommended for good service.
* **English Language**: English is widely spoken across Albania due to schooling and TV influence.
* **Flexibility in Plans**: Maintain flexibility in travel plans as things in Albania may not always run on schedule. Allow extra time for activities and travel.
* **Best Time to Visit**: Summer (June-August) is hot and busy. Shoulder months (March-May and September-October) are recommended for cooler weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices.
* **Avoiding Weekend Crowds**: Visit remote or popular beaches during weekdays to avoid local weekend crowds.
* **Cultural Respect & Dress Code**: Albania is a majority Muslim country; dress modestly, especially when visiting religious sites or when off the beach. Some areas may have signs requiring cover-ups.
