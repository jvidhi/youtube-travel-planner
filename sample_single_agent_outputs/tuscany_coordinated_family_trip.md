## 📅 Day-by-Day Itinerary
### Day 1: Arrival in Athens & Historic Plaka Family Exploration
* **02:00 PM** - **Check-in at The Urban Nest Athens - Suites & Apartments** (`LODGING`)
  *Description*: Check into your spacious family suite at The Urban Nest Athens. Located centrally with ample room for children, this boutique apartment hotel provides the perfect home base for a relaxing and hassle-free Athenian stay.
* **04:00 PM** - **Charming Family Stroll in Plaka District** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Stroll through the pedestrian-only historic streets of Plaka, located right under the Acropolis. Safe, vibrant, and filled with colorful bougainvillea flowers, children will love exploring the small gift shops and sampling local Greek ice cream.
* **07:00 PM** - **Welcome Dinner at Old Ithaki Restaurant** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: Enjoy your first evening with a traditional Greek welcome feast at Old Ithaki. Highly rated and known for its warm, kid-friendly hospitality, you can sample fantastic Greek favorites and local delicacies.

### Day 2: Acropolis Mythology & Sunset at Cape Sounion
* **09:00 AM** - **Mythological Acropolis Guided Tour for Families** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Embark on a family-friendly private guided tour of the monumental Acropolis. A guide will share exciting stories of ancient Greek gods, mythological creatures, and the legendary history of the Parthenon in a way that keeps kids highly engaged.
* **12:30 PM** - **Family Lunch at Dyo Dekares I Oka** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: Recharge after your historical journey with authentic Cretan and Greek dishes at Dyo Dekares I Oka. It features comfortable seating, incredible kid-friendly foods, and legendary hospitality.
* **03:30 PM** - **Excursion to the Spectacular Temple of Poseidon** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Take a gorgeous coastal drive down the Athenian Riviera to Cape Sounion to explore the dramatic Temple of Poseidon perched high above the Aegean Sea. Walk around the cliffside ruins and watch one of Greece's most beautiful sunset vistas.

### Day 3: Ferry to Naxos & Beachfront Playtime
* **07:30 AM** - **High-Speed Ferry to Naxos Island** (`TRAVEL`)
  *Description*: Board a high-speed ferry from Athens to the island of Naxos. Enjoying the ferry network is highly recommended for family island hopping, as children can walk around and marvel at the endless blue Aegean Sea.
* **01:00 PM** - **Check-in at Hotel Naxos Resort** (`LODGING`)
  *Description*: Check-in to the beautiful Hotel Naxos Resort. Set right next to Agios Georgios Beach, this resort offers fantastic family pools, beautiful Cycladic gardens, and is close to everything Naxos has to offer.
* **03:00 PM** - **Agios Georgios Beach & Sunset at Portara** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Head straight to Agios Georgios Beach, famous for having some of the shallowest, calmest, and warmest waters in the Cyclades—ideal for kids. Later, walk up to the historic Portara (giant ancient marble gate) in Naxos Town for a dramatic sunset view.
* **07:00 PM** - **Beachfront Dinner at Nissaki Restaurant** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: Enjoy a memorable family seafood dinner at Nissaki Restaurant, situated right on the sandy shoreline. Dine with your toes in the sand, choosing from fresh caught local fish and traditional Naxian cheeses.

### Day 4: Exploring Traditional Inland Villages & Plaka Beach
* **09:30 AM** - **Naxos Village Road Trip & Halki Exploration** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Rent a family vehicle (highly recommended in Naxos to explore the villages at your own pace) and drive to the historic inland village of Halki. Known for its traditional architecture, old olive mills, and colorful alleyways, it's a delight for the entire family.
* **01:00 PM** - **Lunch at Sarris Tavern** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: Taste authentic Naxian specialities and delicious grilled dishes at Sarris Tavern in Naxos Town. The atmosphere is traditional, lively, and incredibly welcoming to children.
* **03:00 PM** - **Afternoon Play & Rest at Plaka Beach** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Spend a relaxed afternoon at Plaka Beach, known for its endless stretch of golden sands and family-friendly swimming. Perfect for building sandcastles, playing in the gentle surf, and sunbathing.

### Day 5: Travel to Crete & Venetian Harbor Walk
* **08:30 AM** - **Travel to Crete (Chania)** (`TRAVEL`)
  *Description*: Take a quick domestic flight or comfortable ferry ride over to Crete, Greece's largest and most geographically diverse island. Arrive in the beautiful historic region of Chania.
* **01:30 PM** - **Check-in at Kiani Beach Resort Family All Inclusive** (`LODGING`)
  *Description*: Check-in to your family paradise: Kiani Beach Resort Family All Inclusive. This high-end beach resort is specifically designed for families, boasting multiple kid-friendly pools, direct beach access, playground areas, and all-day entertainment programs.
* **04:00 PM** - **Chania Venetian Harbor Family Walk** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Discover the mesmerizing Venetian Harbor of Chania on a family walking tour. See the iconic 16th-century lighthouse, watch street performers, and browse local artisan shops tucked inside centuries-old buildings.
* **07:00 PM** - **Waterfront Dinner at Canale Restaurant Chania** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: Savor authentic Cretan cuisine right along the waterfront at Canale Restaurant. Kids can watch the boats pass in the harbor while enjoying world-class Mediterranean hospitality and kid-approved regional specialties.

### Day 6: Elafonisi Pink Beach Paradise & Farewell Feast
* **09:00 AM** - **Pink Sands Day Trip to Elafonisi Beach** (`ATTRACTION`)
  *Description*: Take a memorable family day trip to Elafonisi Beach, universally famous for its beautiful pink-tinted sands and warm, extremely shallow turquoise waters that form a massive natural wading pool. It is an absolute dream destination for families with kids of all ages.
* **06:30 PM** - **Farewell Cretan Dinner at Irene’s Tavern** (`RESTAURANT`)
  *Description*: End your unforgettable Greek family adventure with a traditional dinner at Irene’s Tavern. Renowned for its home-cooked Cretan hospitality, warm environment, and wonderful kid-friendly gardens.
