## 💡 Practical Travel Tips
* **Safety**: Albania is generally very safe with a low crime rate. Exercise common sense in cities like Tirana. Solo female travelers are safe on northern Albania hiking trails due to well-marked paths and helpful locals.
* **Budget Travel**: Meals range from 5-8 USD. Accommodation around 50 USD/night. Beers €1-3, cocktails €4-7. Local currency (Albanian LE) is generally cheaper to pay with than Euro unless quoted in Euro.
* **Tourism Development**: Tourism is still developing, so expect some "rough and ready" experiences like potholes on smaller roads, slow service, and construction. Public transport can be unreliable.
* **Beaches**: Explore smaller, local beaches outside popular areas like Ksamil, Himara, and Saranda for fewer crowds. Examples: Filicuri, Gajepe (near Himara), Mirror Beach (near Saranda/Niss). Avoid popular local beaches on weekends.
* **Bees**: Albania has a very healthy bee ecosystem, meaning bees are common and can be bothersome during outdoor meals. Locals are accustomed to them.
* **Albanian Cuisine**: Must-try dishes include Barre (meat pie, especially in Valora), Fërgesë (cottage cheese and capsicum), Tavë Kosi (slow-cooked lamb in yogurt), and Trilece (a three-milk sponge cake dessert). It is advisable to avoid chicken, as it is often overcooked.
* **Unmissable Experiences**: Hiking in the Albanian Alps (Valbona to Theth, including a remote ferry ride), visiting the Blue Eye natural spring in Theth, experiencing the interactive "Albanian Nights" play in Tirana, and enjoying sunset cocktails at Saranda Castle.
* **Currency**: Both Albanian LE and Euro are widely accepted. It's generally more affordable to pay in LE unless a price is quoted in Euro. ATM withdrawals incur a fee of 500-800 LE (5-8 USD).
* **Local Interaction**: Albanians are warmhearted; learning a simple phrase like "faleminderit" (thank you) is appreciated. Expect initial reservedness that eases with conversation.
* **Transportation**: Renting a car (approx. €30/day) is the best way to access hidden beaches and hiking trails. Ensure the rental company allows driving on dirt tracks. Petrol is referred to as "benzene." Public buses exist but may not reach all desired locations and can be delayed.
* **Language**: English is widely spoken across Albania due to education and Western media influence.
* **Flexibility**: Maintain a flexible travel plan, as things in Albania may not always run on schedule.
* **Best Time to Visit**: Summer (June-August) is popular but can be very hot and busy, especially mid-July to August. Shoulder seasons (March-May and September-October) offer cooler weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices.
* **Cultural Respect**: Albania is a majority Muslim country. It is important to dress modestly, especially for women, when exploring towns and visiting religious sites. Beach attire is acceptable on beaches, but covering up is expected elsewhere.
